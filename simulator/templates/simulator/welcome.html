Your first few instructions

No need to memorise everything

Select a map and start whenever you’re ready. Your job is to sequence arrivals, release departures and keep traffic separated. Help inside the game pauses the simulation while you check the commands.

Quick-start guide & command examples

1. Select an aircraft and give an instruction

Click a radar target or flight strip to put its callsign in the command box. Type instructions and press Enter or Go. Replace BAW123 in these examples with an active aircraft’s callsign.

CommandWhat it does
BAW123 C 3Climb or descend to 3,000 ft.
BAW123 C 270Turn to heading 270°.
BAW123 C 090Turn to heading 090°. Use three digits for headings.
BAW123 S 180Set speed to 180 knots, within the aircraft’s limits.
BAW123 C WODFly direct to the WOD navigation fix.
BAW123 L 27LClear an arrival to land on runway 27L.
BAW123 GInstruct an arrival to go around.

You can chain instructions: BAW123 C 3 C 270 S 180 assigns 3,000 ft, heading 270° and 180 knots. With C, one or two digits mean thousands of feet, exactly three digits from 001–360 mean heading, and numbers above 360 mean feet.

2. Sequence an arrival

Arrivals hold their assigned altitude until you change it. Use heading, altitude and speed instructions to bring the aircraft onto a runway approach. Runways 27L/27R face 270°; 09L/09R face 090°.

For landing clearance, the aircraft must be at or below 3,000 ft and heading within 60° of the runway direction. Its path must intercept the dashed extended centreline before the runway. Clearance alone does not turn it onto final. Once the approach is captured, it follows the runway and descends automatically. The strip shows AWAITING INTERCEPT or FINAL.

3. Release a departure

Waiting departures appear in the strips. Assign an altitude and runway, then clear takeoff: BAW123 C 5 R 27L T assigns 5,000 ft and runway 27L, then clears takeoff.

Once airborne, route it toward the requested FIX shown on its strip, for example BAW123 C CPT. Waiting aircraft appear on radar once cleared for takeoff.

4. Keep aircraft separated

Aim for at least 3 NM horizontally or 1,000 ft vertically. Red targets and the separation banner identify conflicts, which reduce your score. The simulator has narrow exceptions for established operations on separate parallel runways; crossing traffic and same-runway conflicts remain monitored.

5. Control the pace

Normal (2×) is the default. Use 1× to slow down, or 4×/8× to speed up. Pause stops aircraft and the airport clock; Resume continues. Restart begins a new session. Upcoming strips give advance notice before aircraft become active.

Optional: speak your instructions

Hold Q or the Mic button to speak. Your words appear live. Release to create a command draft, check it, then press Enter or Go. Nothing is submitted automatically.

Example: “Speedbird one two three, descend and maintain three thousand, speed one eight zero, heading two seven zero.” This becomes BAW123 C 3 S 180 C 270. Use “direct Whiskey Oscar Delta” for WOD. Info lists airline names and phonetic callsigns.

Voice needs microphone permission and a supported browser. You can always type instead. The browser’s speech service may process audio remotely.
