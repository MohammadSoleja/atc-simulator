Select an aircraft to fill in its callsign. Commands can be chained. Consecutive waypoint clearances, such as CALLSIGN C FIX1 C FIX2, fly those fixes in order. A later CALLSIGN C FIX3 replaces the whole route. Heading vectors, landing clearances and go-arounds clear the queued route. The current route appears on the flight strip.

Voice: hold Q or the Mic button and speak a callsign followed by instructions. Live speech appears in the command box; release to translate, then check and press Enter. Example: “Speedbird one two three, speed one eight zero, heading {{ airport.wind.heading }}.” Airline names, spelled letters and phonetic letters are supported. Direct instructions use any named fix shown on the selected map. Microphone permission is required; your browser may send audio to its speech recognition service. Voice support depends on the browser.

Live transcription appears in the command box; release Q to review the command draft. Download voice log exports transcripts, alternatives and errors from this tab. Translation errors stay inline and identify the affected instruction. Edit the command box or record again, then check the draft before pressing Enter/Go. Translation does not mean the simulation has accepted a clearance.

Normal (2×) is the default gameplay pace. The clock shows airport time from {{ start_time }} and advances two minutes per real minute at Normal; 1× halves that rate, 4× doubles it, 8× quadruples it. Pause and Help stop both clock and aircraft.

Dashed flight strips preview scheduled traffic {{ airport.traffic.notice_seconds }} seconds before activation (at Normal). Countdown pauses with the game and scales with speed. Departures are blue on radar; arrivals are white. Amber marks selection and red marks conflicts.
